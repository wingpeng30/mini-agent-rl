# v133 sequential research programme

Accepted execution order: AR → AS → AT → AU → AV. Research starts from AM seed42-A (experimental, not published). Goal Z remains the published identity. Each eligible candidate is compared against its immediate parent; rejected or +1 candidates never become the parent of the next stage. This programme does not change product selection.

Common promotion requires >=2 net evidence-supported real-search successes on the existing admitted 29/64 development stratum, no increase in fact-error/unsupported total or invalid/repeated/out-of-budget actions, preservation of Rapana and duke, and at most one lost continuous-search protection among the frozen seven. All other 35 cases retain unconfirmed source status. No new confirmation or expanded data pool. Isolated probe or training-fit gains do not promote a research parent.

AR uses A original legacy action, C lossless addressed evidence plus constrained ordinary action, B same addressed evidence plus constrained evidence selection followed by action. XGrammar enforces syntax and available addresses only. Probe gate before real search, max 840 generations/576 searches, zero updates. Actual plan and manifest are in runs/v133-goal-ar-constrained-evidence-v1. A baseline requires exact historical-token and default-path verification. B must independently improve over C to claim evidence-selection benefit.

AS reuses the existing frozen complete-recovery inventory. Existing gates remain >=8/24 budget restoration, >=16 pairs, >=8 real failed prefixes and entity/role, relation direction/scope, missing-link each >=4 independent cases. Existing closure-v3 reports 1/24 and 10 pairs; skip without candidate expansion or another audit if those counts are confirmed.

AT fixes 16 training-only sufficient-evidence states: the four AM hard cases (Never Say Never collaborator, Ossian Donner father's institution, Terror Mountain director birthplace, Return Of The Seven director full birth date), then the first twelve eligible answer states by stable state_id. Validate with already admitted citations, preserve actual production context and evaluation isolation. Sample each once with four candidates, temperature .7/top_p 1; no search, 64 generation maximum. Correctness is source-supported action correctness including necessary qualifiers, with alias sets frozen before sampling; no gold substring-only rewards. At least four independent mixed correct/incorrect groups are required. Otherwise skip training.

If AT is eligible, paired seed42 A positive-sample action SFT and B group-relative clipped policy update start from the current research parent, share the same sampled pool, original128 trajectories, AM34 states and128 updates. State additions at updates72–87 have coefficient .25. A supervises first correct sampled action per mixed group; B uses all four relative advantages. Nonmixed groups get zero extra term for both arms. LR5e-6/AdamW(.9,.999)/eps1e-8/weight_decay0/clipnorm1/LoRA-only/BF16/no quantization. B clip epsilon .2, KL coefficient .02, reference frozen parent. No sampling retries, parameter scan or extra epochs. If constrained decoding has been accepted, probability computation must replay the identical grammar masks for behaviour/current/reference; unmasked scores are prohibited. AT max640 generations,384 searches,256 seed42 updates. >=2 real-search and >=2 complete-probe gain, partial-search protections and B superiority over A are required to promote RL; otherwise eligible A may promote as SFT with no RL attribution.

AU retains the same corpus, query and weights, scores BM25-v2 top20 using BAAI/bge-reranker-base, returns top3 full original bodies. Freeze model revision/weights/tokenizer and scoring rule before evaluation. Query max64 tokens, document windows384 tokens stride256, include final window; maximum8 windows/doc. Exceeding these fixed feasibility bounds is a recorded skip, not silent truncation or changed parameters. Max window score per document; ties use original BM25 rank then document ID. Input metadata retains original BM25 score; reranking score remains diagnostic. Existing original queries only for offline eligibility, maximum194 unique queries x20 candidates x8 windows. At least four independent confirmed necessary-document promotions from ranks4–20 to top3 and no net decrease in original complete-chain returned cases are required before live A/B64 evaluation. Max512 generations/384 searches. Reranker GPU and latency are reported separately.

AV evaluates cumulative eligible candidate against Goal Z and initial AM on208 existing development/regression questions (dev64 included once). Source-supported metrics use existing valid admission records, with mechanical EM/F1 reported separately. Accepted system components get one leave-one-out64 check each. Accepted training recipes require paired seed43 replay before recommending regression completion; if a training route fails replication, retract it and return to the preceding accepted parent. No best-seed selection. Each old-main, old-regression, Goal Y and AA group may lose at most one supported success, and continuous-search protections at most one. Archived outputs may only be reused after full condition/input provenance verification. Final budgets must be calculated from the actually accepted components and frozen before AV starts; no new confirmation or publication.

The earlier blanket rule “every candidate starts from Goal Z” is amended for this cumulative research sequence: matched arms fork from the same accepted research parent. Goal Z and initial AM are kept for final independent comparison; seed43 replays the accepted training chain. Historical standalone experiments and scores remain unchanged.

Infrastructure/model-identity/GPU/numeric failure stops the whole sequence with preserved scene. Semantic failure or material shortage closes its individual method and continues to the next specified method. Existing user GPU authorization applies; no repeated startup approval.
